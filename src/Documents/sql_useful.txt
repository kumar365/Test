

Query to find salary of employees having Nth Highest salary 

> Select salary from employees order by salary DESC limit n-1,1;


Query to find salary of employees having 4th Highest salary

# Here  we are skipping 3 rows from Top and returning only 1 row after skipping .
> Select salary from employees order by salary DESC limit 3,1;




Query to find names of employees having Nth Highest salary 

> Select emp_name from employees where salary =( Select salary from employees order by salary DESC limit n-1,1);
